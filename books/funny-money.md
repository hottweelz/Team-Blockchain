# Funny Money: Stablecoins, CBDCs and the Tokenization of the Global Economy

**Funny Money** is the first Digital Bytes book volume by **Jonny Fry** and **James Tylee**. It explains how stablecoins,
central bank digital currencies, tokenized deposits, and tokenized assets are reshaping settlement, collateral,
cross-border payments, sanctions, compliance, and financial sovereignty.

## Short Description

The book draws on more than three years of continuous Digital Bytes analysis and companion podcast conversations. It is
written for finance leaders, technologists, regulators, investors, treasury professionals, enterprise operators, and
readers who need a clear map of digital money without a sales pitch for any one token, chain, or institution.

## Why Readers Buy It

- It explains why stablecoins became a real financial infrastructure story rather than only a crypto-market tool.
- It compares stablecoins, CBDCs, and tokenized bank deposits as competing settlement models.
- It connects payment-rail friction, cross-border settlement, regulation, tokenization, and geopolitics in one volume.
- It gives board-level readers a practical way to understand programmable money and the next era of financial rails.
