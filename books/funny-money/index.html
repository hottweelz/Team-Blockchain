Why It Matters

Money is becoming programmable, settlement is moving closer to real time, and institutions are being forced to choose between shaping the new infrastructure or being shaped by it.

  • Understand why stablecoins became the first blockchain use case with genuine financial-system scale.
  • See why CBDCs, tokenized deposits, and private digital money are competing to define the next settlement layer.
  • Track the implications for banks, corporates, regulators, cross-border payments, sanctions, and financial sovereignty.
  • Use a clear, source-aware narrative instead of trying to assemble the story from scattered headlines.

Inside the Book

Funny Money follows the path from legacy payment rails to digital money. It begins with the creditor problem hidden inside ordinary bank deposits, then moves through stablecoin adoption, Terra/Luna's collapse, central bank responses, the digital currency race, tokenized deposits, cross-border payments, sanctions, zero-knowledge proofs, and the future of settlement.

The result is a commercially grounded map of what is changing, why it matters, and which decisions boards, regulators, and builders will need to make as money itself becomes part of the software stack.
