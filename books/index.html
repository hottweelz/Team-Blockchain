Funny Money

Funny Money: Stablecoins, CBDCs and the Tokenization of the Global Economy turns years of Digital Bytes analysis and podcast conversations into one focused volume for readers who need clarity before the next payment-rail shift becomes obvious in hindsight.

Why buy it now?

The book explains why stablecoins moved from crypto infrastructure into mainstream financial plumbing, why central banks are responding with CBDC strategies, and why tokenization is changing settlement, collateral, compliance, and cross-border payments.

It is written for finance leaders, policy watchers, technologists, investors, and operators who want an independent map of digital money without a sales pitch for any one token, chain, or institution.
